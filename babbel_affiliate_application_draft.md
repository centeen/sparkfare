## Promotional property description

> Sparkfare is a flight-deal alert site. We track flight prices daily against each route's own
> 30-day price history and email subscribers when a fare is genuinely, measurably cheaper than
> normal — not just "cheap-looking," but statistically below what that specific route has recently
> cost. Once someone acts on a deal alert and has a trip booked, we shift into "Away Mode": a
> curated set of pre-trip services (travel insurance, luggage storage, mail holding, and similar)
> presented as "everything else, handled," so the traveler doesn't have to hunt for those
> separately before leaving. Language learning fits naturally into that same pre-trip moment for
> travelers heading somewhere their destination's primary language isn't their own.

## How we'd promote Babbel specifically

> Babbel would be added to our "Away Mode" partner list, which currently includes SafetyWing
> (travel insurance), Bounce (luggage storage), and US Global Mail (mail forwarding). This list
> appears in three places: a dedicated `/away-mode` page on the site, a follow-up email sent
> immediately after a user tracks a flight booking, and a second reminder email sent 0–3 days
> before departure. Because we already know the destination for any tracked trip, we can place
> Babbel specifically for international destinations rather than showing it indiscriminately —
> we'd only surface it where it's actually relevant to the trip, not as a blanket upsell.
