In that same spirit of being upfront about the pipeline rather than only announcing finished results, here's what's currently pending, not live yet.

Travel-document processing

We've applied directly to a travel-document and eVisa processing service's own affiliate program and are waiting to hear back. It's a real fit for the "everything else, handled" idea — sorting out entry requirements is exactly the kind of pre-trip logistics Away Mode already covers for insurance, luggage, and mail.

Audiobooks and ebooks

We've also applied to an audiobook and ebook service through their affiliate network. Long-haul flights are a natural occasion for exactly this — several of our own destination guides (Tokyo, Sydney, Bali) are long enough hauls that "something to actually read or listen to" is a real, unglamorous part of the trip.

Neither is live yet

Both applications are genuinely pending — no decision back either way. We're not counting on approval, and we won't add either link to Away Mode or anywhere else until it actually comes through with a real, working tracking link, same discipline as every partner already on the list.
