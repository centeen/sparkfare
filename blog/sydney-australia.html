Sydney, Australia: the long-haul sprint that justifies itself

Destination guide

Sydney's opera-house skyline meets world-class beaches and culinary innovation. Rare pricing windows make this 9,000+ mile journey surprisingly accessible—worth a look before rates reset. Like every priced route, Sydney earns a deal badge only when a fare falls below its own 30-day median by more than twice its usual day-to-day price swing, and only once the route has at least 10 days of price history spanning 14 days or more.
