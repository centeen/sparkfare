A generic "get travel insurance" checklist is easy to skim past. Here's the same three real partners — SafetyWing, Bounce, US Global Mail — applied to four specific trips we've already written guides for, so it's clear what each one is actually for rather than a box to tick.

Lisbon, Portugal

Tram 28 and the ferry to Belém make for a full day out — worth having SafetyWing in place before you're navigating cobblestones you're not used to. If your flight lands early or your outbound is late, Bounce means you're not hauling a bag through Alfama for six hours first.

Marrakech, Morocco

Confirm SafetyWing's coverage actually extends to North Africa before you book — not every travel policy does by default. If mail piles up at home for the length of a proper Morocco trip, US Global Mail means nothing important sits in a mailbox untouched.

Tulum, Mexico

Mexico's healthcare is genuinely good, but a coverage gap still hurts — SafetyWing is the baseline here. Between cenote stops and beach days, checking a bag with Bounce on either end of the trip beats dragging it through sand.

Prague, Czechia

A longer Central European stint often means more mail accumulating at home than a weekend trip would — US Global Mail covers that. SafetyWing stays the same non-negotiable it is everywhere else on this list.
