Luxor, Egypt: a destination guide

Destination guide

Ancient temples, modern convenience: Luxor delivers five-star archaeological access at fraction-of-Europe pricing. Nile-side luxury under $100/night. Sparkfare tracks Luxor looking for significant price drops below its own 30-day average. Full math in how we rank deals.

What it actually costs on the ground

  • Valley of the Kings + Karnak Temple complex cost $30 combined entry; world-class Egyptology museums included at near-zero marginal cost
  • 3-star riverfront hotels with private Nile balconies run $60–80/night; domestic flights from Cairo ($25–40) beat overland transport by 6 hours
  • Your USD/EUR stretches 3-4x further than Mediterranean rivals: restaurant meals $3–6, private Nile felucca cruises $15/hour, guided tomb tours $20/person

Before you lock in dates

Before departure, secure travel medical coverage for North Africa (cholera/typhoid precautions available), arrange trusted pet or property oversight, and lock down international data: Luxor's 4G exists but roaming costs spike.
