Luxor, Egypt: ancient temples at fraction-of-Europe pricing

Destination guide

Ancient temples with modern convenience — Luxor delivers real archaeological access at a fraction of what similar depth costs in Europe. Sparkfare files Luxor under Cluster 4 ("Visual Clickbait") — always featured on the board when there's data, regardless of price, and never carrying a "deal" badge. See why some destinations always make the board for the reasoning.

What it actually costs on the ground

  • The Valley of the Kings and Karnak Temple complex together cost $30 combined entry.
  • 3-star riverfront hotels with private Nile balconies run $60–80/night, and domestic flights from Cairo ($25–40) beat overland transport by 6 hours.
  • Restaurant meals run $3–6, private Nile felucca cruises run $15/hour, and guided tomb tours run $20 per person.

Before you lock in dates

Secure travel medical coverage for North Africa, arrange trusted pet or property oversight, and lock down international data before you land — Luxor's 4G exists, but roaming costs can spike. See our Away Mode checklist for the real partners we use for this.
