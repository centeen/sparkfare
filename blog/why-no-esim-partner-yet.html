Why we don't have an eSIM partner on Away Mode yet

Away Mode

Read enough of our destination guides and a pattern shows up: nearly every one mentions confirming a local SIM or roaming plan before you land. It's a real, recurring need — and yet there's no eSIM link on our Away Mode checklist today. Here's honestly why, rather than pretending it's not a gap.

What actually happened

We applied to an eSIM affiliate program early on. It came back a soft decline — not a rejection of the idea, just a "not the right moment yet," with an explicit invitation to reapply once our audience and content had grown. We took that at face value rather than treating it as a dead end.

What's pending now

We've since applied directly to a different eSIM provider's own affiliate program and are waiting to hear back. Same rule as every other partner on this site: we don't add a link until an application is actually approved and there's a real, working tracking URL in hand — a guessed or placeholder link would silently break commission tracking, which is worse than just not having the partner yet.

Why we're writing about a gap instead of hiding it

It would be easy to just not mention eSIMs until we have a partner to show. We'd rather be upfront that this is a known, real gap we're actively working on, consistent with how we handle every other pending application — see how we decide whether a partner makes the list for the fuller version of this same discipline.
