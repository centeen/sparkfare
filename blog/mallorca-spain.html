Mallorca, Spain: cliffs and coves before the peak-season markup

Destination guide

Mediterranean limestone cliffs meet a genuinely underpriced shoulder season — Mallorca between April and May runs well below peak rates. Sparkfare files Mallorca under Cluster 2 ("Shoulder-Season Cliffs"), meaning a fare needs to clear at least 15% below its own 30-day average before we badge it a deal — full math in how we rank deals.

What it actually costs on the ground

  • Tramuntana mountain hikes and pristine coves cost virtually nothing to access — Cala Formentor is a €3 bus ride from Palma.
  • Local seafood and Mallorcan wine average €12–18 per person at beachfront restaurants, matching coastal Italy at half the markup.
  • Spring weather (65–72°F) means archaeological sites and cathedral interiors stay uncrowded.
