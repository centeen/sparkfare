Trip protection has a clock: why timing matters if you want it

Away Mode · Last reviewed 2026-10-08

This post is education only. We are not recommending any plan or provider and do not link to any.

The clock

Some trip protection benefits are only available if you buy within a set window after your first payment toward the trip. Squaremouth, a travel insurance comparison site, says these time-sensitive benefits are usually available within 10 to 21 days of the first trip deposit, depending on the policy. Examples it names include cancel for any reason coverage, pre-existing medical condition waivers and financial default protection. Once the window closes, you no longer qualify for those benefits.

What counts as the first payment

Usually the first money you spend on the trip, often the airfare or a hotel deposit. That date is the start of the clock, not the day you started planning.

What to do

  • Note the date of your first trip payment when you make it.
  • If you think you may want any of these benefits, read your own plan's exact deadline before the window closes. It varies by plan, so check the policy wording.
  • If you are not sure whether a benefit applies to you, ask the insurer or the plan's customer service. We cannot advise.

If you decide you do not want trip protection, nothing here changes that. The point is to make it a decision, not something that lapsed.
