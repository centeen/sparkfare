## Two real routes, compared 2026-09-12

| | Awin (merchant 15132) | Direct network (nordvpn.com/affiliate-signup) |
|---|---|---|
| Account needed | **None — reuses the Awin account already open** (from SimpliSafe/Airport Reservations/Timekettle) | New account, with its own password, on NordVPN's own affiliate platform |
| Commission | Flat **40% on every sale** (confirmed directly on Awin's own merchant page) | Tiered: **100%** on new 1-month signups, **40%** on new 1-year/2-year signups, **30%** on all renewals (confirmed directly on NordVPN's own affiliate page) |
| Cookie | 30 days | 30 days |
| Friction | Low — apply from inside the Awin account you already use | Higher — full new account with a fresh password, a 3-step signup flow |

**Recommendation: apply via Awin.** The two programs converge to roughly the same real payout for
any customer who buys a 1-year or 2-year plan (40% either way) — the direct network's "100%"
headline only actually pays out more if a meaningful share of referred customers pick the 1-month
plan specifically, which isn't something to bank on before there's real traffic. Reusing the
already-open Awin account avoids creating and managing yet another affiliate-network login, the
same reasoning already applied to AAA (via CJ) and YourMechanic (via Impact) in the vehicle-care
research. This is a recommendation, not a rule — if you'd rather chase the higher short-plan rate
on the direct network, the account-creation flow there requires setting your own password, which
isn't something I can do on your behalf.

## Awin route — what to do

1. Log into the Awin account already open for this project.
2. Go to merchant 15132 ("NORDVPN (US & CA)") and click **Join Program**.
3. Based on how the other Awin applications in this project have gone (SimpliSafe, Airport
   Reservations, Timekettle), this is typically a single click against the account's existing
   business profile — no separate long-form application observed for those. If Awin does prompt
   for a program-specific promotional description, use the same paragraph drafted for Babbel:
   Sparkfare is a flight-deal alert site that shifts into an "Away Mode" partner checklist
   (travel insurance, luggage storage, mail forwarding, and similar) once a user has a trip
   tracked — a VPN/data-security tool fits naturally into that same pre-departure moment for
   travelers connecting to public Wi-Fi abroad.

## Direct network route — if you'd rather go this way instead

`nordvpn.com/affiliate-signup` is a real, live, 3-step signup flow (confirmed 2026-09-12): step 1
asks for account type (Personal/Company), name, email, and a password. Creating that account and
choosing a password is something you'd need to do yourself — I can't see or draft what steps 2/3
ask until an account exists to get past step 1. Once you're through, the promotional-description
paragraph above should work there too.
