## Key National Findings for 2027

### 1. The Zero-PTO Long Weekends
Three major national windows require zero days of leave to take a 3- or 4-day trip:
* **Thanksgiving Weekend (Nov 25–28, 2027):** 4 consecutive days off (Thursday Thanksgiving through Sunday) with 0 PTO days used for employers providing the standard day-after-Thanksgiving holiday.
* **Independence Day (July 3–5, 2027):** July 4 falls on a Sunday in 2027. Under official federal OPM rules, the holiday is observed on Monday, July 5—giving all workers an automatic 3-day summer weekend (Saturday to Monday) for 0 PTO days.
* **Christmas Weekend (Dec 24–26, 2027):** Christmas Day falls on a Saturday in 2027. Observed on Friday, Dec 24, creating an immediate 3-day winter weekend for 0 PTO days.
* **Memorial Day (May 29–31, 2027) & Labor Day (Sept 4–6, 2027):** Standard 3-day holiday weekends (Saturday to Monday) for 0 PTO days.

### 2. High-Efficiency Bridge Opportunities (4x+ Return)
* **The Spring Mega-Break (Memorial Day Bridge):**  
  Take 4 PTO days (June 1–4) after Memorial Day Monday to get **9 consecutive days off** (May 29 to June 6).
* **The Late-Summer Break (Labor Day Bridge):**  
  Take 4 PTO days (Sept 7–10) following Labor Day to get **9 consecutive days off** (Sept 4 to Sept 12).
* **The Thanksgiving Extended Break:**  
  Take 3 PTO days (Nov 22–24) leading up to Thanksgiving to turn the holiday into **9 consecutive days off** (Nov 20 to Nov 28).
