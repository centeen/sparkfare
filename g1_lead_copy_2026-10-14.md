# G1 Long Leave: exact user-facing text (for the Design Council Guard review and walkthrough)

Handoff task 3a (D-006). Copied verbatim from the code on `main` as of 2026-10-13; nothing is paraphrased. Source files:
`src/longLeave.js` (the three lead items), `long-leave.js` (the renderer, the heading and the 30+ option),
`src/leaveReady.js` and `leave.html` (the `/leave` flow), `away-mode.html` (the `/away-mode` surface).

**State today:** `ENABLE_LONG_LEAVE` is `"false"` in production, so none of this is visible. With it off the `30_plus` option is not
offered and a client that sends it is stored as `2+ weeks`. Nothing in this file has been switched on (task 3b is held for the Hub).

## 1. The lead: heading and three items

Rendered by `renderLead()` in `long-leave.js`. A heading, then an ordered list. Item title, then action text, then links.

**Heading (an `h2`):**

> If you will be away for 30 days or more

**Item 1 (key `mail`)**

- Title: `Decide what happens to your mail`
- Action: `USPS Hold Mail can hold your mail at your local Post Office for up to 30 days. For a longer absence, USPS offers mail forwarding instead. A virtual mailbox is a third option: it receives your mail, scans the envelope, and forwards what you ask for.`
- Links (in order):

| Label | Destination | Opens | `rel` | Sponsored? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `USPS Hold Mail` | `https://www.usps.com/manage/hold-mail.htm` | new tab | `noopener noreferrer` | no |
| `USPS Forward Mail` | `https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm` | new tab | `noopener noreferrer` | no |
| `US Global Mail (partner link)` | `/out/us-global-mail?src=leave_long` | new tab | `sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer` | **yes** |

The third link is built only when `us-global-mail` is in the live partner list (it is today). The label's name comes from the partner row
(`US Global Mail`). If that partner were not live, the item would show the two USPS links only.

**Item 2 (key `vacancy`)**

- Title: `Ask your insurer about an empty home`
- Action: `Many homeowner policies change coverage if a home is unoccupied for a set period. Ask your insurer what their rule is before you go.`
- Links: none. No partner, no tracking, no insurer link (D-002 / ROADMAP Step 19).

**Item 3 (key `water`)**

- Title: `Water`
- Action: `Shut off the main water supply, or ask someone to check the home while you are away.`
- Links: none.

## 2. Where and when the lead renders

| Surface | Condition | What the visitor also sees there |
|---|---|---|
| `/leave` (result card, above "Still open") | the answer to "How long will you be away?" is **A month or more**, flag on | summary line, the other open items, the partner disclosure line (below) |
| `/away-mode` (a block above the checklist, `#long-leave-lead`) | signed in **and** saved trip length is `30_plus`, flag on | the normal Away Mode checklist and partner list |

Not shown on any other length, signed out on `/away-mode`, or with the flag off. On `/away-mode` the page's usual partner list is
unchanged below it.

**On `/leave`, the lead replaces the standard "Sort your mail" and "Water" entries** (and their "done" versions) so nothing appears
twice. The rest of the list is unchanged.

## 3. Other text the lead sits next to

**The `/leave` question and option (unchanged by G1; "A month or more" now stores `30_plus` when the flag is on):**

> How long will you be away?
> Options: `A weekend` · `1 to 2 weeks` · `A month or more` · `Skip`

**`/leave` note for a signed-in visitor whose trip length is already saved (flag on, weekend or 30+ only):**

> We are using the trip length saved to your account. [Change it]

**`/leave` summary line (above the lead), where N counts the lead's three items plus the other open items:**

> `1 thing left to sort` / `N things left to sort` / `Nothing left to sort from your answers.`

**`/leave` partner disclosure (shown only when some link on the page is sponsored):**

> Sparkfare may earn a commission if you buy through a partner link, at no extra cost to you. Read our disclosure.

**`/leave` footer line:**

> This is a simple list built from your answers. It is not advice, and it does not assess your home.

**The trip-length option the flag adds** (home signup bar, `/widget`, `/account`; appended to the trip-length `select`):

> `30+ days` (stored as `30_plus`)

## 4. The standard `/leave` items the lead suppresses (for comparison)

So the council can see what a 30+ visitor does *not* get:

- Mail, answered "no": title `Sort your mail`; action `Ask the post office to hold your mail, or have a neighbor collect it.` plus, for a month or more with the partner live, ` For a long trip, a virtual mailbox can also open, scan and forward mail.`; links `USPS Hold Mail` and `US Global Mail (partner link)` (`/out/us-global-mail?src=leave`).
- Water, answered "no": title `Find your water shutoff`; action `Locate the main valve (often where the water line enters the home, near the meter) and check you can turn it by hand.`

## 5. Events (not copy, listed because the walkthrough asks)

With the flag on only: `leave_started`, `leave_long_branch_shown` (`surface`: `leave` or `away_mode`), `leave_trip_length`, `leave_completed`;
partner clicks carry `src=leave_long`. With the flag off none of these are logged.

## 6. Things the Guard review should look at (observations, not decisions)

1. **USPS wording.** "up to 30 days" and "mail forwarding" were checked against usps.com on 2026-10-13 (`src/longLeave.js`, comment above
   `USPS_HOLD_MAX_DAYS`). The handoff asks for a re-check on the day of the flip.
2. **Item 2 names insurance but links nowhere.** It is a question to put to the visitor's own insurer, in line with D-002.
3. **The only sponsored link is the virtual mailbox,** and it is the third link of item 1. The disclosure sits at the bottom of the `/leave`
   result card, not directly above the lead on `/leave`; on `/away-mode` the page's own partner disclosure applies. This placement is the
   thing most worth the council's eye (ROADMAP step 16 asked for disclosures next to the links).
4. **"Water" assumes a main shutoff and that the visitor is able to use it.** The `/leave` flow has no "I rent" or "I do not own" branch.
5. **Anonymous visitors on `/leave`** are asked the length every time and nothing is stored.
