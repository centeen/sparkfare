## 1. The three lead items (`buildLongLeaveLead()` in `src/longLeave.js`)

Rendered as a numbered list, in this order, under the heading in section 2.

### Item 1: mail (`key: mail`)
- **Title:** Decide what happens to your mail
- **Body:** USPS Hold Mail can hold your mail at your local Post Office for up to 30 days. For a longer absence, USPS offers mail forwarding instead. A virtual mailbox is a third option: it receives your mail, scans the envelope, and forwards what you ask for.
- **Links, in order:**
  1. "USPS Hold Mail" -> `https://www.usps.com/manage/hold-mail.htm` (external, not sponsored)
  2. "USPS Forward Mail" -> `https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm` (external, not sponsored)
  3. "US Global Mail (partner link)" -> `/out/us-global-mail?src=leave_long` (sponsored; opens in a new tab; `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer"`). Appears only while the `us-global-mail` partner is live in the registry.

### Item 2: empty home (`key: vacancy`)
- **Title:** Ask your insurer about an empty home
- **Body:** Many homeowner policies change coverage if a home is unoccupied for a set period. Ask your insurer what their rule is before you go.
- **Links:** none. No partner, no tracking (ROADMAP step 19).

### Item 3: water (`key: water`)
- **Title:** Water
- **Body:** Shut off the main water supply, or ask someone to check the home while you are away.
- **Links:** none.

## 3. Where the lead appears, and who sees it
- **`/leave`:** shown when the visitor answers "A month or more", signed in or not. Items replace the standard mail and water entries for that run. Other answers get no lead.
- **`/away-mode`:** shown at the top only for a **signed-in** user whose saved trip length is `30_plus`. Anonymous visitors never see it there.
- The USPS 30-day figure was re-checked on usps.com on 2026-10-13 ("a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days").
