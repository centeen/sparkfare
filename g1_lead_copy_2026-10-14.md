# G1 Long Leave: verbatim user-facing copy (for Design Council review)

Exported 2026-10-15 from `src/longLeave.js`, `long-leave.js` and `leave.html` on `main`, and cross-checked against the live
`GET https://sparkfare.com/api/long-leave` response. Text is exactly as shown to a visitor; nothing here is paraphrased.
**Status: `ENABLE_LONG_LEAVE` is `"true"` in production (see `g1_long_leave_report_2026-10-15.md`).**

## 1. Where the copy appears

| Surface | Condition |
|---|---|
| Home signup bar, `/widget`, `/account` trip-length select | flag on: one extra option |
| `/leave` | flag on and the visitor answers "A month or more" (or has a saved `30_plus` length) |
| `/away-mode` | signed-in visitor with saved trip length `30_plus` |

## 2. Trip-length option

- Option label: `30+ days` (stored token `30_plus`). Added by `long-leave.js`; the other options are unchanged.

## 3. /leave length question

- Question: `How long will you be away?`
- Answers: `A weekend` · `1 to 2 weeks` · `A month or more` · `Skip`
- Saved-length note: `We are using the trip length saved to your account.` with a button `Change it`.

## 4. The lead block

Heading (`long-leave.js`): **If you will be away for 30 days or more**

### Item 1: mail
- Title: **Decide what happens to your mail**
- Text: USPS Hold Mail can hold your mail at your local Post Office for up to 30 days. For a longer absence, USPS offers mail forwarding instead. A virtual mailbox is a third option: it receives your mail, scans the envelope, and forwards what you ask for.
- Links:
  1. `USPS Hold Mail` -> https://www.usps.com/manage/hold-mail.htm (not sponsored)
  2. `USPS Forward Mail` -> https://www.usps.com/manage/forward.htm (not sponsored)
  3. `US Global Mail (partner link)` -> `/out/us-global-mail?src=leave_long` (**sponsored**; shown only while the partner is live)

### Item 2: empty home
- Title: **Ask your insurer about an empty home**
- Text: Many homeowner policies change coverage if a home is unoccupied for a set period. Ask your insurer what their rule is before you go.
- Links: none. No partner, no tracking (ROADMAP step 19, D-002).

### Item 3: water
- Title: **Water**
- Text: Shut off the main water supply, or ask someone to check the home while you are away.
- Links: none.

## 5. Facts behind the copy

- "up to 30 days": USPS says "a minimum of 3 days and a maximum of 30 days" and, for longer, to "sign up for a forwarding service"
  (usps.com/manage/hold-mail.htm, re-checked 2026-10-13). The number lives in `USPS_HOLD_MAX_DAYS`.
- The only commercial element is the third mail link, a sponsored link to US Global Mail, disclosed in its label.
- No urgency language, no insurance recommendation, no price or savings claim.

## 6. Questions for the Council

2. Is "Ask your insurer about an empty home" sufficient while insurance referral licensing (step 19) is open?
3. Should the third item cover more than the main water supply (heating, appliances), or stay this short?
