Methodology, Data Sources & Sample Limits

This report provides an empirical benchmark based on observed flight search history. To ensure objectivity and truth in reporting, Sparkfare adheres strictly to the following parameters:

  • Data Source: Cached round-trip economy flight search queries from the Travelpayouts flight data cache. Fares reflect lowest observed prices across search sessions, not live inventory quotes for arbitrary dates.
  • Observation Period: Continuous tracking from September 6, 2026 to October 22, 2026 across 880 distinct US city-pair routes (22 origins × 40 destinations).
  • Deal Floor Rule (D-021): A fare is badged as a "Deal" only when it is at least 15% below its 30-day median AND has accumulated at least 10 distinct observation days across a minimum 14-day span. Fares failing either criteria are labeled "Typical" or "Insufficient history".
  • Coverage Disparity: 12 established hubs (JFK, EWR, LAX, ORD, MIA, BOS, SFO, DFW, IAH, IAD, ATL, SEA) hold sufficient depth to compute honest medians. 10 newer origins (AUS, BWI, CLT, DEN, DTW, FLL, LAS, MSP, PHL, PHX) return limited cache data; Sparkfare reports this thin coverage openly rather than fabricating baselines.
Strict Consumer Protections: Sparkfare does not sell or book travel. We make no future price predictions, state no ideal purchase dates, and make no claims regarding whether holiday airfares will rise or fall. Airfares fluctuate dynamically based on airline yield management, seat inventory, and seasonal demand. Observed search lows provide historical context, not a price guarantee.
