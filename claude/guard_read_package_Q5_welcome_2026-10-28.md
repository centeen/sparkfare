## 1. The two emails

**Verification email** (replaces "Verify your Sparkfare account"; transactional; sample: `verification.html`)
- Subject: **Confirm your email to start your fare alerts**
- Body: "One tap confirms your email and starts your fare alerts from {City}." ({City} is the home airport's city, or "your home airport" when unknown)
- Link text: **Confirm my email**
- No referral block (removed on the Hub's M2 condition), no partner, no fare. No unsubscribe line or postal address (transactional).

**W1 welcome** (sent once, right after the person confirms; transactional stream per the addendum; samples: `welcome_with_deal.html/.txt`, `welcome_typical_fare_LAX.html`, `welcome_no_fare.html/.txt`)
- Subject: **You're in: fares from {City}, each morning**
- Preheader (hidden): **Today's lowest fare from {City}, and how to change the pace.**
- Line 1: **You'll get fares from {City} ({IATA}), at most one email a day.**
- Card (only when a fare can be shown): "{City} → {Destination}", "$N round trip", the basis line, "Fare data retrieved {date, time and zone of the home airport}."
  - Basis line appears **only for a verified deal**, and is the record's own text, for example "22% below 30-day median, 31 observations".
  - A typical fare shows price and the "retrieved" line only.
- Button: **See this route** → `/flight/{origin}/{destination}` (the route page; no Aviasales link, no `/out/` link, no partner link anywhere in the email).
- Secondary button: **Watch a route you care about** → `/watchlists?origin={IATA}`
- Pace line: **Prefer one email a week?** + link **Change how often** → `/account`
- Footer: unsubscribe link, the standard one-line "deal-information service" statement, and the postal address (added by hand, because the transactional stream drops the shared footer).
- No fare to show: the card and "See this route" are replaced by **Your first digest arrives tomorrow morning.**

## 4. Where the build differs from the addendum text

1. **Time promise (Q7).** Line 1 says "at most one email a day" with no "about 6:30 a.m. your time", as the addendum says to do while Q7 has not shipped. The 6:30 version is kept in the content file (`line1_with_time`) and is not used.
2. **Postal address in a transactional email.** The addendum asks for the standard footer with a postal address and also says stream 1. The sender drops the shared footer for stream 1, so W1 prints the address itself when `EMAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS` is set.
3. **The new verification copy is behind the same flag** as W1, so the existing live subject and body do not change until the flip.

## 5. Questions

1. **Preheader when there is no fare.** The preheader says "Today's lowest fare from {City}…", but the no-fare variant has no fare. Options: (a) keep, (b) use "Your first digest arrives tomorrow morning, and how to change the pace." when there is no fare. Recommendation: (b).
2. **"Each morning" without Q7.** Until the local-morning send ships, the digest arrives at 08:00 UTC (04:00 on the east coast, 01:00 on the west coast in this season's time). Is "each morning" acceptable in the subject now, or should it wait for Q7 / use "daily"?
3. **Transactional stream.** W1 follows the person's own action but is welcoming, not required for the service. Is "transactional" the right classification (it is exempt from the postal-address block and the circuit breaker), or should it count as a lifecycle email?
4. **"See this route" vs the first deal.** The card shows the lowest fare, not necessarily a deal. Is "fares from {City}" with a typical fare shown (sample: `welcome_typical_fare_LAX.html`) acceptable, or should the card only appear for a verified deal?
5. **Verification subject** "Confirm your email to start your fare alerts": the alerts do not start until the next digest. Is "start" fair, or "set up"?
