Prompts by persona (Fogg B=MAP). Signal prompts for Dana (motivated and able: just

Attachment (Park and MacInnis). Attachment grows when a brand enables, assures and
gratifies the self. Sparkfare enables (tools that do the work), assures (basis, sources,
privacy) and gratifies (the anticipation: the place, the plan, the spark). Prominence comes
from being useful on a cadence the person already has: the daily digest, the PTO year, the
school calendar, the leave-by dates.

percentage, and starts from things the person has really done (dates chosen, trip length
set). Shown only for trips of 7 days or more.

5.4 Voice and tone
Five rules. Say the basis. Numbers lead, words explain. Plain words, short sentences. Calm
verbs ("view", "watch", "check", "plan"). Warm, not cute.
