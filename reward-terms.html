Referral Reward Terms

How our feature rewards work.

When you invite friends to Sparkfare, you unlock features that make your flight tracking even more powerful. Here are the plain-language terms for how rewards are earned and redeemed.

1. Earning Referrals

A referral is considered "confirmed" when a friend signs up using your unique referral link and successfully confirms their email address (double opt-in). Self-referrals (signing up with your own email addresses or from the same device) are not permitted and will be automatically rejected.

2. Reward Tiers

  • 1 Referral (Extra Origin): You can select a second home airport to track deals from.
  • 3 Referrals (Early Bird Alerts): You will receive deal alerts before they are broadcasted to the main free list (typically around 07:00 UTC).
  • 5 Referrals (Early Access): You will get early access to test out new beta features before they launch to everyone else.
  • 10 Referrals (Founding Member): You will receive a permanent "Founding Member" badge on your account profile, recognizing your early support of Sparkfare.

3. Important Details

Rewards are digital feature unlocks only (no cash value, physical goods, or paid services). Sparkfare reserves the right to modify these tiers or revoke rewards if abuse is detected.
