Amendments to steps 22-23 (paid value must come from other features) and 32 ("faster alerts" no longer a valid referral reward). Commit: docs/roadmap-acquisition-steps (ROADMAP.md).
- DECISION 2026-10-03: Brand-differentiation steps 59-62 added (Spark Check stamp and hotlinkable chart, open scoring code with auto-disputed "report a wrong deal", Sparks rating and "No sparks today" note, public track record).
